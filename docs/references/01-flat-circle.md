# Reference 1: Flat Circle

- **Shape:** flat disk
- **Why it's useful:** the basic case. Increases spread evenly around each round keep the piece flat.
- **Status:** unverified

## Source A: 14-bead start

- **Source:** [Peyote Stitch Master Class: Circular Peyote, Inspirational Beading (2016)](http://inspirationalbeading.blogspot.com/2016/04/peyote-stitch-master-class-circular.html)
- **Convention:** the 14-bead ring is the "foundation", and rounds are numbered after it.

| Round | Beads per gap | Beads added | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | – | 14 | Ring, even count |
| 1 | 1 | 7 | Every other ring bead is skipped, so there are 7 gaps |
| 2 | 2 | 14 | Increase: a pair in every gap |
| 3 | 1 | 7 (as summarized) | Into the pairs. **Check:** splitting 7 pairs usually gives 14 gaps |
| 4 | 3 | 21 | 3 beads per stitch, 7 stitches |
| 5+ | 2 | 14 | More increases as needed |

The source notes that the number of rounds decides whether the piece stays round or turns into a 7-pointed star, and recommends using fewer beads rather than more.

## Source B: 3-bead start

- **Source:** [Circular Flat Peyote Stitch Instruction, BeadJewelryMaking](https://www.beadjewelrymaking.com/Arts_and_Craft_Idea/circular_flat_peyote_stitch_instruction.html)
- **Convention:** the 3-bead ring is the start, and rounds are numbered after it.

| Round | Beads per gap | Beads added | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start | – | 3 | Ring |
| 1 | 2 | 6 | A pair in each of the 3 gaps |
| 2 | 1 | 3 (as summarized) | **Check:** after 3 pairs there would usually be 6 gaps |
| 3 | 2 | 6 | |
| 4+ | alternating 1 and 2 | – | Add 2 instead of 1 when a gap gets too wide, and spread the increases evenly |

## Open questions for the format

- Both sources give fewer beads in the round after the pairs than splitting would normally need. Either the summary is wrong, or the pair is treated as a single unit for one round before it splits. The format has to support both (`pair` and `split`).
- Source B has no fixed schedule ("when the gap gets too wide"). For the model, we need a fixed schedule, and the beader's judgment has to become explicit counts.
