# Reference 2: Rivoli Bezel (Cup)

- **Shape:** short tube whose edges curl inward on both sides into a cup that holds a round crystal (rivoli)
- **Why it's useful:** shows that **no increases + smaller beads + tight thread** make a curved 3D shape. Bead size has to be part of the model, not just bead count.
- **Status:** unverified

## Source A: 12 mm rivoli, 32-bead ring

- **Source:** [Creating a Peyote Bezel, Around the Beading Table, © 2012 Deborah Roberti](https://www.aroundthebeadingtable.com/Tutorials/PeyoteBezel.html)
- **Fits:** 12 mm round rivoli
- **Thread:** FireLine, pulled tight throughout

| Round | Bead type | Beads added | Beads per gap | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 11/0 Delica (cylinder) | 32 | – | Starting ring in 2 alternating colors, which makes a ridge around the middle |
| 3 | 11/0 Delica | 16 | 1 | Plain peyote, no increase |
| 4 | 15/0 seed (round) | 16 | 1 | Smaller beads start the front cup. Tight tension |
| 5 | 15/0 seed | 16 | 1 | Front cup done. The rivoli goes in here |
| 6–7 | 15/0 seed | 16 each | 1 | Back side, worked from the other edge of the ring |

The round numbers here use the ring-is-rounds-1-and-2 convention. The page summary numbered them differently, so check against the source.

## Source B: size chart for other rivoli sizes

- **Source:** [How to Make Peyote Stitch Bezels, Artbeads](https://artbeads.com/blog/how-to-make-peyote-stitch-bezels/) (also [diagram PDF](https://artbeads.com/content/tutorial-pdfs/abt-20140814-02-open-back-bezel-diagram.pdf))

| Rivoli | Ring (11/0 Delica) | Extra 11/0 rounds | Front 15/0 rounds | Back 15/0 rounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 mm | 26 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| 12 mm | 30 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 14 mm | 36 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 16 mm | 42 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 18 mm | 46 | 1 | 3 | 3 |

Sources A and B give different ring counts for a 12 mm rivoli (32 vs 30), so the count depends on bead brand and tension.

## Useful for the model

- The ring count grows with the rivoli's circumference: going from 10 mm to 18 mm adds about 2.5 beads per extra mm. With 11/0 Delicas at about 1.6 mm wide, the ring circumference is ~N × 1.6 mm, a little bigger than the stone, so it should be checkable.
- How much the cup curls depends on the **ratio of bead widths** (15/0 at about 1.3 mm, 11/0 at about 1.6 mm). This gives us a direct test for step 4.
